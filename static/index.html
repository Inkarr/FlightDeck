Log a trade

Send trades to UEX

Your buys and sells can go into your UEX Trade Log, which also helps keep UEX prices up to date. You need two things from uexcorp.space:

  1. App token: open My Apps (uexcorp.space/api/apps), create an app (any name, e.g. Flightdeck) and copy its token.
  2. Secret key: open your UEX profile and copy your secret key (40 characters).

UEX only takes trades from accounts verified with RSI. Both keys stay on your PC (my_uex.json); the tablet never sees them.
